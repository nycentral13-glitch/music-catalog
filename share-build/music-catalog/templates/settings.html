Start automatically at login

When enabled, the catalog starts itself in the background every time your Mac logs in — no Terminal needed. Just open localhost:5001 in your browser whenever you want it.

Uses a macOS LaunchAgent stored at ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.vinyl-catalog.server.plist

{% if autostart %}
Auto-start is active. The app will launch silently at every login and log output to flask.log in your music-catalog folder. To stop it running right now without disabling auto-start, open Terminal and run:
launchctl unload ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.vinyl-catalog.server.plist
{% endif %}
