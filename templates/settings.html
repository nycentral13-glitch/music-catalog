Start automatically at login

When enabled, the catalog starts itself in the background every time your Mac logs in — no Terminal needed. Just open localhost:5001 in your browser whenever you want it.

Uses a macOS LaunchAgent stored at ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.vinyl-catalog.server.plist

{% if autostart %}
Auto-start is active. The app will launch silently at every login and log output to flask.log in your music-catalog folder. To stop it running right now without disabling auto-start, open Terminal and run:
launchctl unload ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.vinyl-catalog.server.plist
{% endif %}

Discogs API Token

Update or replace your Discogs token. Get one free at discogs.com → Settings → Developers.

Sync to Discogs Collection

Push all records in your local catalog to your Discogs account as a cloud backup. Enter your Discogs username (the one shown at discogs.com/settings/profile).

⏱ This process takes 20–40 minutes for a large collection. The app syncs one record per second to stay within Discogs' rate limit, and automatically pauses 65 seconds if it gets throttled. Leave this page open and the sync will run to completion in the background.
